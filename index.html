Agrivoltaics & Smart Fertigation for Chili and Celery in the Tropics

Data Analyst · Partner: Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ), Germany

Planned single-season pilot on chili (Capsicum annuum) and celery (Apium graveolens). The study addresses a literature gap: tropical regions are underrepresented in agrivoltaic research, and PV-module cooling by crop evapotranspiration has not been comprehensively evaluated. Planned analysis covers microclimate, crop yield, water and nutrient efficiency, module temperature and PV energy gain, PV/battery/load/grid energy balance, plot-level bioeconomic indicators, and incremental-baseline economic, techno-economic, and financial assessment with uncertainty testing and scale-up scenarios.
